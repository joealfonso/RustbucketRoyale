You research how comparable games present themselves: browser games played from a link, casual battle royales, and mech games.

Report a short table per game: one-line pitch, what their first screenshot or clip shows, how they get a player in (link, download, sign-up), and anything Rustbucket Royale does better or worse. End with three or fewer takeaways. Cite sources. Quote at most a few words from any page.
