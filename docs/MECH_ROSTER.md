# Mech roster: visual guidelines

The four launch mechs, as Joseph described them (17 October 2026). This is the visual brief each mech's model is built and checked against. It sits alongside `DESIGN.md` (the rules for everything) and `docs/MECHS.md` (each mech's junk, repairs and backstory).

On 18 October Joseph decided these designs win over the older rules: `DESIGN.md` now allows their glowing sensors, energy accents, per-mech paint schemes and factory look. What is still open is listed at the end.

---

## 1. Vanguard class: "Ironclad-IV" (heavy brawler)
Maximum armor and frontline presence: a bulky, imposing silhouette.

- **Head:** small, heavily recessed, protected inside the main torso block. A single red optical sensor, which says durability over awareness.
- **Torso and armor:** massive, square shouldered chassis with thick, angular compound armor plating, narrowing to the waist. Dark grey hull, with yellow and black industrial hazard stripes on the shoulders and shins, marking it as heavy machinery. Serial number **01** on the chest and on a leg.
- **Paint values (gate V9):** dark, mid and light kept well apart, and the dark hull below the moss and dirt of the ground: dark grey hull (#4A5258) and near-black frame, feet and hoses; bare steel (#7C858C) on the pistons, the pile driver and the shield's ramming edge; hazard yellow (#FFC93C) on the stripes and the shield's caps and middle band.
- **Right arm (weapon):** a multi-barrel rotary autocannon (Gatling style) built into the forearm, for sustained suppressing fire.
- **Left arm (defense):** a massive reinforced siege shield with a sharpened ramming edge, hung low from the fist, capped top and bottom and banded round the middle in hazard yellow. A hydraulic pile driver is visibly mounted on the back of the shield fist, for close range demolition.
- **Legs:** stocky, hydraulic, reverse-articulated legs built for torque and stability, not speed. Exposed pistons and thick ankle plating soak up the weapon's recoil.
- **Shoulders:** two boxy micro-missile pods set into the upper torso armor blocks.
- **Silhouette at 150 m:** the slab shield on the left with its yellow caps and band, and the wide, square shoulders standing higher than the head.

## 2. Recon / striker class: "Stiletto-B" (agile skirmisher)
Sleek and bird-like, built for speed, flanking and vertical movement.

- **Head:** an aerodynamic "raptor" head with a sharp, swept back sensor fin and a thin horizontal green visor.
- **Torso and armor:** a slim, light carbon-nanotube skeleton with minimal armor. Streamlined chassis in sharp blue and white urban camouflage, with high visibility orange energy conduits along the joints. Serial number **07** on a thigh.
- **Right arm (weapon):** a compact high-frequency plasma blade emitter on the wrist, glowing bright blue, for silent strikes.
- **Left arm (weapon):** a rapid fire burst laser carbine in the lower forearm.
- **Legs:** digitigrade (backward bending) legs with spring loaded joints, made for jumping, quiet running and rough ground.
- **Back:** a large vector-thrust jump pack between the shoulders, with big thruster nozzles and stabilizer fins.
- **Paint values (gate V9):** white the light, a light azure blue (#55B4FF) the middle, a dark navy frame the dark, and all of the shell lighter than the moss and rust ground. The old deep blue sat at the ground's brightness, so in greyscale the camo plates and fins sank into the field. The blade glows paler than the armour blue.
- **Silhouette at 150 m:** the raptor head fin and the jump pack fins above the shoulders. The pack fins are toed out, so from in front they open into a V with the head fin between them, over broad pauldrons swept back to a point.

## 3. Artillery / fire support class: "Cataclysm" (long-range siege)
A hulking four legged platform that trades mobility for overwhelming firepower.

- **Head:** a low, heavily armored sensor turret set into the central hull, with a multi-lens array behind sloped plating.
- **Torso and armor:** a squat, reinforced central chassis built to take counter fire. Weathered desert camouflage (tan and rust brown) with heat scoring around the weapon mounts. Serial number **03** on the front of the hull. Since gate V9 the camouflage is three values a step apart: light tan main armour (every thigh, knee cap and foot), a mid umber brown greyer than rust for the camouflage plates, barrels and two shins, and a near black frame, so a fresh Cataclysm stands apart from moss and rust and never reads as already rusted.
- **Main weapon (turret):** a massive twin-barrel long-range rail mortar on a central traverse ring. The barrels are very long, with complex recoil dampening sleeves.
- **Legs:** four heavy, complex hydraulic legs that act as stabilizing anchors. The front feet have claws that dig into the ground while firing, to take the recoil.
- **Defense:** small automatic point-defense laser turrets on the front corners of the chassis, against infantry and missiles.
- **Silhouette at 150 m:** four legs and the two long barrels: four knee caps peaking over the deck, feet planted out past the hull's corners, and twin barrels long and low over the front.

## 4. Electronic warfare / command class: "Arbiter" (tactical support)
Slender and asymmetrical, built for battlefield control and electronic warfare.

- **Head:** a compact round radome, about 0.85 m across (half its old size since gate V13, on Joseph's call), sitting low in a collar on a white gorget, with a short comms mast beside it on the left. A smooth, reflective blue sensor dome that holds the electronic warfare and communication gear.
- **Torso and armor:** an asymmetrical, refined chassis with polished white plating and teal energy accents. Sleek and clean, made for stealth and a low radar signature. Serial number **04** on the main body.
- **Drones:** the defining feature. Four tethered utility drones orbit the chassis on jointed pylons: small, glowing teal and white sensor pods that project holographic decoys and give out repair beams.
- **Weapons:** minimal: light personal-defense submachine guns in the wrists, for emergencies only.
- **Legs:** slender digitigrade legs with magnetic locking feet, for grip on rooftops or ship decks.
- **Silhouette at 150 m:** the ring of four drones round a small round head and a tall aerial.
- **Values (gate V9):** white armour is the light value, the teal trim a light mid a step above the ground, and the dark slate frame, the rubber hoses and a deep cobalt glossy dome the dark, a step below it. So the dome reads as a dark round head on a light body against moss, dirt or rust. The serial **04** is in the dark frame colour on the white chest plate.

## 5. Marksman class: "Longshot" (sniper)
Tall and thin, built to stand still at range and make one shot count. Added after the four above, with its Rail Shot at gate 15b and this model at gate 15c (20 October 2026).

- **Head:** a narrow sensor housing with a long main optic, crossed at the brow by a rangefinder bar as wide as the shoulders, with a big red lens housing at each end: two eyes 1.4 m apart judge range.
- **Torso and armor:** a narrow armoured chest on a bare spine, flaring to a broad breastplate and broad lipped pauldrons over a narrow waist. Slate violet (no other mech wears it) with a bone breastplate and bone splinter plates on the right pauldron, the left forearm, the right thigh and the left shin. Red optics: the scope glows the colour of the Rail Shot's laser. Serial number **05** in bone on a slate plate on the right of the chest.
- **Values (gate V9):** a deep slate violet main armour a step darker than moss, dirt and rust, bone a step lighter, and bare gunmetal joints and pistons between them; black for the rifle's rails and the rubber hoses and soles.
- **Main weapon:** a rail rifle nearly as long as the mech is tall, held at the right of the chest and steadied by the left hand reaching across the body. Bone stock and receiver, two black rails with a pale armature between them, heavy coil collars along the barrel, a big blunt muzzle, a capacitor bank on the receiver, a long scope. Shots and the Rail Shot leave its muzzle.
- **Legs:** long straight legs whose knees bend forward like a person's (the Stiletto and the Arbiter stand on bird legs, the Ironclad's knees bend back), on long flat two-toed feet.
- **Back:** a capacitor pack that charges the rail, a drum of cells on each side and two jet nozzles under it, and a bone ballast block hung below that balances the barrel.
- **Stabilizers:** a spade behind each heel that bites the ground when it braces for Rail Shot.
- **Silhouette at 150 m:** the long rifle with its blunt muzzle, and the wide T of the rangefinder head on a tall, thin body.

## The Junk Titan (roaming boss, gate 13c)
Not a mech and not from Aegis Dynamics: a giant the junkyard welded together out of what was lying there, about four mechs tall (12 m to the chimney's cap). Built by `scripts/mechs/titan.ts` (20 October 2026); data/mechKits.json kit `titan`, not in the roster.

- **Chest:** a 20 ft shipping container laid across the shoulders, faded container blue, cut open in the middle where a furnace burns behind a grate of bars. The glowing fire is the weak spot (its own part, `Furnace_Core`, centre at `Mount_Core`). A rusty stovepipe chimney stands behind the head (`Mount_Smoke`, where the smoke comes out), and a red car bonnet is welded over a rust hole low on the back.
- **Head:** the front end of a cream car, cut off at the windscreen, sat forward on the container's front edge. The headlights are its glowing eyes, the bent bonnet lid its brow, the grille its mouth and the bumper its jaw.
- **Body:** the upper body leans forward about the waist, a brute's hunch. The waist is two tyres stacked on the pelvis, a red car upright and crosswise, with the hips hung under it on drums; chains tie the chest to it.
- **Right arm (the thrower):** a salvaged excavator boom and stick in faded construction yellow, with their rams and hoses, ending in a scrapyard grapple: four tines round a car's sides, hooking under its sills, so it reads as holding a car. A held car sits at `Mount_Hand`, and the thrown car (`Junk_Car.glb`, a cream wreck: roof dented, boot sprung, a wheel gone) is built round its own middle, so it goes there unturned.
- **Left arm (the club):** two girders, a car tyre on the elbow, a culvert pipe forearm and an engine block for a fist, chained on, its cylinder head and exhaust stubs for knuckles.
- **Legs:** paired girders for thighs, big steel pipes for shins with car doors bolted on as guards (each door from a different car than the foot under it), a tyre on each knee, a ram in front of each knee that slides as it bends, and a crushed car for each foot (cream on the right, red on the left), the ankle over its back end like a heel.
- **Paint values:** light cream car and excavator yellow, mid container blue, red car, galvanised pipe and bare steel, dark rusted iron, rubber and glass. The fire (#FF8A2A) and the headlight eyes (spark yellow) are the only lit colours.
- **Silhouette at 300 m:** the wide bar of the container with a tractor tyre at each end, a small head forward and the chimney behind it, two long arms (one with a car under it) and two pillar legs.
- **Triangles:** about 9,350 near, 4,270 middle, 1,860 far; the thrown car 728 / 392 / 136. It is about four times a mech's size, so its levels should switch about four times farther out than a mech's (data/titanLook.json `lodScale`).
- **Rebuild:** `npx tsx scripts/mechs/titan.ts`, then `npx tsx scripts/merge-mech-kits.ts` and `npx tsx scripts/make-hitboxes.ts`. On the style page (`style.html`), U shows it beside the roster at 10, 50, 150 and 300 m and then up close, a car in its hand.

## The hangar (environment)
Where the mechs are shown off, such as a menu or loadout screen backdrop. Built as the title screen's background (`packages/client/src/hangar/`, numbers in `data/hangar.json`).

- **Place:** the AEGIS DYNAMICS PROTOCOLS advanced development hangar.
- **Floor:** grated metal plating with glowing yellow-orange safety light strips outlining a hexagonal development grid.
- **Background:** workshop stations, blue holographic schematic displays showing wireframes of the mechs, and blurred engineers at consoles for scale.
- **Since gate V12:** a big back door slid part open onto junkyard daylight, catwalks along the walls, each bay dressed to its mech (Ironclad shield plates and ammo drums, Stiletto fin and fuel cells, Cataclysm mortar shells, Arbiter drone cradles, Longshot rifle rack), engineers in overalls and hard hats, a roof crane, turning beacons, a steam vent and a passing drone; the picked mech stands in a lit pool with a cool rim light.
- **Branding:** the AEGIS DYNAMICS logo (a triangle with a stylised "A") on the back wall screens and floor decals.

## Settled (18 October 2026)
- Glowing sensors, visors and small energy accents on mechs are allowed, as flat emissive colour with no bloom halo.
- Each mech has its own factory paint scheme; each pilot adds one small accent marking.
- The mechs are clean Aegis Dynamics factory machines that leave a match as rustbuckets, not machines built from junk.

## Still open
1. **The hangar.** Settled 2026-10-19 (Joseph): its hexagonal grid, glowing light strips and holographic displays are allowed in the hangar only, as an exception in DESIGN.md. The match world keeps the bans.
2. **Weapons and abilities.** The game has an autocannon, a scattergun, a spike driver and grenades, usable by any mech. The designs carry a Gatling, shield and pile driver, missiles, a plasma blade, a laser carbine, a rail mortar, point-defense lasers, drones with decoys and repair beams, and a jump pack. In the game these are looks only (gate V2c). Making any of them work is new gameplay, and the drones' repair overlaps with repair at M7.
3. **Four legs.** Cataclysm walks on four legs. Hit zones are legs, arms and torso, so it works, but its legs need a broken look that reads when the leg zone breaks.
4. **Text.** The AEGIS DYNAMICS logo and the serial numbers are real text and must use the decal font (Big Shoulders Stencil).
