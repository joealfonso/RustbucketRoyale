# Mechs

Each mech is built from named junk, shows its repairs, and has a one line backstory. Asymmetry needs a reason (a patch, a swapped part), never randomness. Every hero mech starts as hand drawn thumbnail silhouettes, and the scan of the chosen one is stored next to its entry.
