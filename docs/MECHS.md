# Mechs

Each mech is a factory machine from Aegis Dynamics: it drops into the junkyard clean, in its own factory paint, and leaves the match as a rustbucket. Damage, rust and lost parts tell its fight. Asymmetry needs a reason (a shield arm, a weapon arm, an electronics mast), never randomness. Every hero mech starts as hand drawn thumbnail silhouettes, and the scan of the chosen one is stored next to its entry.

The four launch mechs and their visual brief are in `docs/MECH_ROSTER.md`.
