# Asset log

Every asset that enters the repo gets a row. Steam asks about AI generated content, so this record has to exist and be accurate.

Rules: AI-generated models are allowed, including mechs, as long as they pass the asset intake checklist in DESIGN.md like any other model (parts, damage states, LODs, budgets, naming, master toon material) and the generator's terms allow commercial use. Every use of AI is recorded here, accurately, because Steam asks.
